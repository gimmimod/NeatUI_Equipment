description=[h2]👔 The Wardrobe[/h2]
description=Save an outfit once, put it back on whenever you like — even when the clothes are not on you any more.
description=[list]
description=[*] 🧍 See it before you save it: your character in 3D wearing exactly what will be saved, with a name and a symbol of your choice
description=[*] 📂 Click a saved outfit to open it: the outfit in 3D on your own character, and the shirt button to put it on
description=[*] 🛡️ Beside it, protection and temperature show that outfit's own defence and warmth, part by part, and replace saves what you have on over it, name and symbol kept
description=[*] 🔎 It finds every piece: already on you, in your inventory, or in any container within reach
description=[*] 🎨 Colour and pattern are remembered, so a blue shirt comes back blue
description=[*] 🎯 An outfit is exact — what it contains goes on, what it does not comes off
description=[*] 🎒 Your bags are never taken off. Two ticks decide, outfit by outfit, whether bags and the belt with its hotbar are part of it
description=[*] 👟 Changes from the feet up, one part of the body at a time, taking things out and putting them away one container at a time
description=[*] 💬 Missing a piece? It tells you which one and puts the rest on anyway
description=[*] 🔄 Container swap, on by default: each piece you take off goes back where things of its kind already are — ideally the container its replacement came from — and stays in your inventory when there is no such place. A note over your head says where things went, and nothing ever ends up on the floor, on a mannequin or in a fire
description=[*] 🔽 The arrow in its header rolls the Wardrobe up to a title bar, the Neat Rocco's UI way
description=[*] 💾 Stored as descriptions, not links — outfits survive a reload and the original garment being destroyed
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]✨ The panel[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] 🧍 Your character in 3D, centred, turning as you dress and undress
description=[*] 🟧 Slots down both sides, the same rounded squares as your hotbar
description=[*] 👐 Both hands as round slots in the bottom corners
description=[*] 🧦 Layered clothing shows as small chips beside the slot — click to fold them out
description=[*] ⌨️ The hotbar below the doll, so you can see what is clipped where
description=[*] 🌍 Translated into all 26 languages the game ships with
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🎛️ Four buttons in the title bar[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] 🔗 [b]Chain[/b] — unhook the panel, move and resize it freely, click again to snap it back
description=[*] 👁️ [b]Eye[/b] — hide the equipped section in the inventory list
description=[*] 👕 [b]Shirt[/b] — strip everything in one press; press again and it all goes back on
description=[*] 🪝 [b]Hanger[/b] — open the Wardrobe
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🖱️ Controls[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Click the figure[/b] beside your inventory to open and close the panel — on the left of the inventory, or on the right if you prefer it there (Options → Mods)
description=[*] [b]Drag and drop[/b] to equip, unequip, swap hands or clip to the hotbar
description=[*] [b]Right-click[/b] any slot for the usual item menu
description=[*] [b]Drag the title bar[/b] to move the panel once unhooked, the [b]corner grip[/b] to resize it
description=[*] Assignable hotkey under [i][NeatUI Equipment][/i] in Options → Key Bindings
description=[*] 🎮 Controller supported — pick the button in Options → Mods
description=[/list]
description=

description=[h2]💾 Compatibility[/h2]
description=✅ [b]Made for CleanUI.[/b] The figure button lives on the edge of the CleanUI inventory, the panel hangs off it and follows it around, and the slots are Clean HotBar slots — one interface, not two mods sitting side by side. CleanUI's own shirt button steps aside: hiding equipped items is the eye's job here.
description=📐 [b]It matches your inventory.[/b] Hooked on, the panel stretches down to meet the bottom of your inventory window, so resizing CleanUI resizes it too.
description=🎨 [b]Or looks exactly like it.[/b] Tick [i]Look like CleanUI[/i] in Options → Mods and the panel and the wardrobe take CleanUI's own frame and background opacity. Otherwise set the background opacity yourself with a slider.
description=🎒 [b]Inventory Tetris is supported.[/b] With Tetris on, this panel takes the place of the equipment window Tetris brings with it — one panel on the edge of your inventory instead of two fighting over the same spot — and drag and drop works across both: a garment dropped on the grid lands in the square you dropped it on.
description=🖥️ Client-side code only, so there are no server checksum issues.
description=🤝 Designed to work alongside the other Neat mods.
